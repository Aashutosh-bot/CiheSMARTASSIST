CIHE Fees and Payment Guide

Tuition Fee Payment
Tuition fees are payable by 10 January for Semester 1 and 20 June for Semester 2 each year. Fees can be paid via the Student Portal using credit card, bank transfer, or through an approved payment plan.

Payment Plans
Domestic students facing financial hardship may apply for a payment plan through Student Financial Services, allowing tuition to be split into installments across the semester.

Refund Policy
Students who withdraw before the payment due date (10 January for Semester 1, 20 June for Semester 2) are eligible for a full refund of tuition fees for that unit. Withdrawals after the due date are not eligible for a refund except in cases of approved Special Circumstances.
