CIHE Fees and Payment Guide

Tuition Fee Payment
Tuition fees are payable by the census date each semester. Fees can be paid via the Student Portal using credit card, bank transfer, or through an approved payment plan.

Payment Plans
Domestic students facing financial hardship may apply for a payment plan through Student Financial Services, allowing tuition to be split into installments across the semester.

Refund Policy
Students who withdraw before the census date are eligible for a full refund of tuition fees for that unit. Withdrawals after the census date are not eligible for a refund except in cases of approved Special Circumstances.
