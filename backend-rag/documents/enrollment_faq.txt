CIHE Enrollment FAQ

Q: How do I enroll in a unit at CIHE?
A: Students enroll through the CIHE Student Portal. Log in with your student ID, navigate to "Unit Enrollment," select your intended units for the semester, and confirm your enrollment before the census date.

Q: What is the census date?
A: The census date is the final date by which a student must be officially enrolled in a unit to be liable for fees for that unit. Withdrawing after the census date will still incur the unit fee.

Q: Can I change my enrollment after the semester starts?
A: Yes. Students may add or drop units up until the census date without academic penalty. After the census date, withdrawal requires approval from Student Services.
