CIHE Assessment Policy Summary

Late Submission Policy
Assignments submitted after the due date without an approved extension will incur a penalty of 5% of the total available marks per day, including weekends, up to a maximum of 5 days late.

Extensions
Students may request an extension of up to 5 working days by submitting an Extension Request Form through the Student Portal at least 24 hours before the due date, along with supporting documentation.

Academic Integrity
All submitted work must be the student's own, with all sources properly cited using APA referencing. Use of Generative AI tools must be clearly acknowledged where permitted by the unit's assessment brief.
